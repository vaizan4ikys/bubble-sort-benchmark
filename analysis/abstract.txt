Bubble Inc. must choose one of three Java bubble-sort implementations, judged only by running time. We ran a pre-registered, full-factorial experiment: three algorithms, six input orders and six sizes (100 to 20 000 elements), ten fresh JVMs per condition, identical data for all algorithms. Two of six hypotheses were supported and four falsified, notably because early-stopping WhileNeeded was 7–16 % slower than plain PassPerItem on reversed input, an effect that other JDKs and computers did not reproduce. A steady-state re-measurement, prompted by an audit, left the verdicts unchanged. WhileNeeded is fastest in 25 of 30 conditions and at most 16 % behind the best, while PassPerItem is up to 40 000 times slower. As the pre-registered criterion was not met, we recommend WhileNeeded on a post-hoc criterion.
