Bubble Inc. must choose one of three Java bubble-sort implementations, judged only by running time. We ran a pre-registered, full-factorial experiment: three algorithms, six input orders and six sizes (100 to 20 000 elements). Each of the 108 conditions was measured in ten fresh JVMs with 30 timed batches each, always on identical data for all algorithms. Four of six hypotheses were partly falsified. On reversed input the early-stopping WhileNeeded is 7–12 % slower than the plain PassPerItem, and UntilNoChange is up to 26 % slower than PassPerItem on random input. Nevertheless WhileNeeded is fastest in 24 of 30 conditions and otherwise close, whereas PassPerItem is up to 39 000 times slower on sorted input. We recommend WhileNeeded.
