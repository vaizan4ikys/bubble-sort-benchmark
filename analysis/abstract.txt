Bubble Inc. must choose one of three Java bubble-sort implementations, judged only by running time. We ran a pre-registered, full-factorial experiment: three algorithms, six input orders and six sizes (100 to 20 000 elements), with ten fresh JVMs and 30 timed batches per condition and identical data for all algorithms. Two of six hypotheses were supported, four partly falsified, notably because early-stopping WhileNeeded is 7–12 % slower than plain PassPerItem on reversed input. Follow-up experiments (garbage collectors, JDK versions, element types, JIT levels, tiny arrays, cold starts, JMH) show that this loss is a C2-compiler effect specific to JDK 25/26 and cheap comparisons. We recommend WhileNeeded: fastest in 24 of 30 conditions, while PassPerItem is up to 39 000 times slower.
