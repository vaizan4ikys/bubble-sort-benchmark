Bubble Inc. must choose one of three Java bubble sorts, judged only by running time. We ran a full-factorial experiment, with hypotheses committed to git beforehand: three algorithms, six input orders, six sizes (100 to 20 000 elements), ten fresh JVMs per condition, identical data for all algorithms. Two of six hypotheses were supported, three falsified and one inconclusive; notably, WhileNeeded was 7–12 % slower than PassPerItem on reversed input, which other JDKs and computers did not reproduce. A re-measurement prompted by a warm-up problem left every verdict unchanged. In it, WhileNeeded is fastest in 25 of 30 conditions (24 in the main data) and at most 16 % slower than the best; PassPerItem is up to 40 000 times slower. As the registered criterion was not met, we recommend WhileNeeded on a post-hoc criterion.
