Bubble Inc. must choose one of three Java bubble sorts, judged only by running time. We ran a full-factorial experiment, with hypotheses committed to git beforehand: three algorithms, six input orders, six sizes (100 to 20 000), ten fresh JVMs per condition, same data for all. Two of six hypotheses were supported, three falsified and one inconclusive; WhileNeeded was 7–12 % slower than PassPerItem on reversed input, which other JDKs and computers did not reproduce. A re-measurement after a warm-up problem left every hypothesis-level verdict unchanged. There WhileNeeded is fastest in 25 of 30 conditions (24 in the main data), at most 16 % slower elsewhere (reversed input); PassPerItem is up to about 40 000 times slower. We recommend WhileNeeded, on a post-hoc criterion.
